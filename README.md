# GrokHub

GrokHub is a desktop assistant powered by Grok, for Windows and Linux. It runs on your computer, works in your files and apps with your permission, and keeps going in the background from the system tray.

## What it does

- **Chat with Grok** about anything, or hand it real work: coding, research, files, and tasks on your computer.
- **Works on your desktop, safely.** Grok can open apps, type and click for you, but anything risky (spending money, sending messages, deleting files, passwords) always waits for your OK.
- **Pulse** brings you a personal feed and ideas, things it noticed you might want done.
- **Automations** run jobs on a schedule, like a weekday summary at 9 AM.
- **Remembers you.** It learns your preferences over time, and the memory stays on your computer.
- **Privacy first.** Your chats, memory and keys are stored on your computer.

## Get it

- **Windows:** download the `GrokHub-Setup` installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/blackviperxiii-ui/GrokHub/releases/latest) and run it.
- **Linux (Arch / CachyOS):** grab the Linux download from the [latest release](https://github.com/blackviperxiii-ui/GrokHub/releases/latest).

On first launch, sign in with your SuperGrok account and you're ready. GrokHub keeps itself up to date.

## Beta

Want new features early? On Linux, turn on **Settings → General → Labs → Beta channel** (it builds from a source clone; Windows stays on stable for now). You can switch back to stable anytime.
