Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve. Then name the smallest change that would solve it.

Use your words only. Do not paste the ask back. The exception is names. Echo product and skill names exactly as the ask wrote them, because a paraphrased name points at nothing.

If the user asked for a unique name (a project, a package, a branch family, a product), propose 2 or 3 and wait for their pick before you create directories or start workers. A name is expensive to change once paths and briefs carry it.

Do not wait for approval on the restatement itself. State it and keep going. The user can redirect. Reuse the restatement in subagent briefs and in the PR's `## Why`.
