# Unslop

Strip AI tells; keep meaning and tone. Rule ids stable; gaps OK if a rule drops.

3 **-ing fluff** (highlighting, ensuring, showcasing): cut or ground.
5 **Vague credit** ("Experts believe"): cite or delete.
7 **AI lexicon** → plain: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape/tapestry (abstract), pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant.
8 Fancy is (serves as, stands as, boasts, features) → is/has.
9 "Not just X but Y" → one claim.
10 Forced threes → natural count.
11 Synonym cycling → one term.
12 False ranges → list items.
13 No em dashes; periods/commas only (no parens, en dashes, hyphen-dashes).
14 Colons for lists/examples only, not mid-sentence pivots.
15 No bold spam on names/acronyms.
16 "**Label:** restates line" → prose. Bold lead + period + new detail OK.
17 Sentence-case heads.
18 No decorative emoji.
19 Straight quotes.
20 Bot phrases (hope this helps, let me know, of course, certainly, smoking gun): cut.
22 Sycophancy: answer directly.
23 in order to→to; due to the fact that→because; drop "important to note".
24 Hedge stacks → one modal.
25 Empty endings → fact or next step.
26 Metaphor nouns → concrete: substrate, wedge, vector (way), locus, vantage, nexus, primitive (noun), harness/surface/bedrock/scaffolding (metaphor), modality, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet (metaphor), evacuate (move), endgame, north star, flywheel.
27 Feel → mechanism/number; cut lines that fit any other docs unchanged.
28 Split dense sentences; one idea each.
29 Active voice; name the actor.
30 Adverbs → stronger verb or measured delta.
31 utilize/leverage→use; facilitate→help; numerous→many; in the event that→if.
32 Mannered prose → literal.
33 Restore articles/verbs; spell out arrows/abbreviations.
