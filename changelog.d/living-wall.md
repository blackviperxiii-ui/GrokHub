- Settings → Behavior → **Living wall**: while the cabin is closed, the desktop app paints a new Imagine cover every three hours (two low-quality 1k stills from a fixed set of scenes) and pins it to the Imagine gallery, keeping twenty and dropping the oldest first. It waits out Halt, quiet hours, a running chat or game, a scratch chat and a Make in progress, and a failed cover tries again in 15 minutes. Off for new installs; an `app.json` the cabin saved with `imagineWall` on keeps painting. The Imagine page shows a new cover as it lands.
