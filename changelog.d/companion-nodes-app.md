- Companion devices, part 3: Devices → Companions starts pairing, shows the code and the command to type on the companion, and lists each paired phone or computer with a box for everything it offers. Nothing is lent until you tick it and press Allow. An allowed companion's tools arrive in chats like any connector's, with its key sealed; a wake brings the app forward in a new chat, and what a companion heard is sent in the open chat.
