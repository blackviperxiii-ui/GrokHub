- Companion devices, part 2: `grokhub-hub node` turns a phone (Android in Termux), another computer or a headless box into a companion. It pairs with the code the app shows, lends only what its person offers (camera, screen, location, text messages, speech, panels, its own MCP servers and skills, a wake event), and prints a line for every tool the app calls. Removing a companion in the app now ends its open poll at once.
