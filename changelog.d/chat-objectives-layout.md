- Settings → Appearance → **Objectives beside chat**: chats show the open objective's steps (the current one marked, done ones struck through) and up to five recent chats on the left, and a three-reading PC snapshot on the right, in place of the companion dock. The left column opens from 900px wide and the snapshot from 1280px. Off by default; auto setup offers it on a screen 2560px or wider, and a pick in Appearance is kept as your own.
