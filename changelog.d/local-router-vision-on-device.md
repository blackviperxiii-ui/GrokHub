- On-device vision (local router, step 7, second part): a Gemma 4 model on this computer now reads the picture in a chat that runs here. Download its vision projector (Settings → Download models → Other) and the router loads it with the model and its draft model when your newest message carries a picture; when the three don't fit, a smaller Gemma 4 whose projector is on disk answers, the line under the reply says why, and your pick comes back once the chat is quiet.
