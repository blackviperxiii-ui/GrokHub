- `scripts/install.sh` now installs the prebuilt app by default, with nothing to compile: the AppImage on Linux x86_64 and the app from the .dmg on Apple silicon Macs. It checks the release's signed `SHA256SUMS` with minisign or OpenSSL 3, and the download against it, before it installs anything. It also runs piped from `curl`, takes `--version` and `--from-dir` (files already downloaded), and builds from the clone only with `--from-source`.
- New `scripts/install.ps1` does the same on Windows: it checks the signature with minisign (the one on PATH, or minisign 0.12 checked against a pinned SHA-256), checks the installer's hash, then runs it silently.
