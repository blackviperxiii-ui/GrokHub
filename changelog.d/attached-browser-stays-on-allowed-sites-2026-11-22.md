- Your browser: when Loadout works in your own signed-in browser, it uses only page tools (no running code, no cookies, no files put into pages, no console messages from earlier pages), works in a new tab it opens rather than one you had open, and doesn't see your other tabs' addresses. If a click takes the tab to a site you didn't allow, the app keeps that page from the model until the tab is back on an allowed site, however long the answer, and a page's own text (a dialog or a tab title) can't make it count as allowed. Snapshots it takes are saved beside the browser profile, not in your project.
