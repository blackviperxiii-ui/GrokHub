- Settings → Pulse: Done for you, off by default. "Pause an automation that keeps failing" pauses a job after 5 failed runs in a row, at most 5 a day, never during quiet hours, Halt, a running chat, a waiting decision or a game, and only after you've tapped Pause it once and approvals aren't on "Ask me first". It never pauses for a key, credit, rate-limit or network error, and a job you turned back on waits for 5 more runs. Its Pulse card says what it paused and why, with Undo (turns it back on and keeps any edits you made while it was paused) and Stop doing this. A failing automation's own card now has Pause it beside Find out why.
