- Chat can look up mods again in the desktop app: it searches a game's sanctioned sources, reads a mod's files and what it needs, and lists what is installed, through the Mods page's own client, cache and egress guard. Asking it to set up a mod puts an Install card, marked "asked for in chat", on the Mods page and nothing more. The download waits for your click on the card, and the files wait for the sheet that lists them. Dismiss clears the card, and a newer ask replaces it.
