- Settings → Local AI has a "Use Local AI on this computer" switch. An older version of the app could leave Local AI switched off with no way to turn it back on; this switch turns it back on, and turns it off for anyone who wants nothing to run on the on-device model. Off frees the model's memory as soon as any answer in progress finishes, and the page, the tray and the Home tile say Off. Typo tidy waits while Local AI is off.
